Avoid confusion

Common misconceptions

“A Service runs my application.”

No. Pods run containers. A Service supplies a stable virtual endpoint and selects eligible Pods.

“Ingress works after I create the YAML.”

An Ingress resource needs an Ingress controller. For new designs, also evaluate the Kubernetes Gateway API.

“A Secret is automatically encrypted.”

Secret data is base64-encoded, not inherently encrypted. Configure encryption at rest and least-privilege RBAC.

“Kubernetes adds persistence to a container.”

Container filesystems are ephemeral. Stateful data needs an appropriate volume, claim, backup, and lifecycle plan.
